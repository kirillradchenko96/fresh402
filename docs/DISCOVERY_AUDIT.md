## Coinbase Bazaar: eligibility is not indexing

The public merchant API and filtered search each returned one resource: **/v1/check**. Its price is still 5000 atomic USDC, but its description, service name and schema are older than the live v2 challenge. **/v2/extract** and **/v2/smart-diff** were absent from the complete merchant result (pagination total=1). They are discoverable through Fresh402's own OpenAPI, MCP and manifest and are **technically eligible based on locally valid advertised extensions**, not verified indexed by CDP.

CDP documents asynchronous indexing after successful settlement carrying both resource and Bazaar extension, public search/merchant discovery, and a nonpaying validation endpoint. A validator or HTTP 402 success does not prove catalog inclusion. No payment was made to force indexing. See [Get discovered](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/x402/seller/get-discovered) and [public discovery interfaces](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/x402/buyer/discover-services).
